ANATOMY OF A ViMi HOODIE

01

Self-Supporting Double Layer Hood

Constructed with dual-layer 450 GSM cotton so the hood stands upright structurally around the neck instead of collapsing flat.

02

Dropped Shoulder Architecture

Cut 4 inches wider than traditional tailoring. Creates that relaxed, aesthetic silhouette favored in modern luxury streetwear.

03

High-Density Loopback French Terry

Zero polyester, pure 100% Indian combed cotton. Pre-shrunk and bio-washed for cloud-like interior softness without shedding fuzz.

04

Snug 2x2 Reinforced Ribbing

Wrist cuffs and bottom hem engineered with heavy-gauge ribbed elastane to prevent loose sagging over years of heavy rotation.
